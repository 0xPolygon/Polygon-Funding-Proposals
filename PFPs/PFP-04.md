# CTB Season 3 Funding Strategy (2025Q3 & Q4)

## Abstract

The Community Treasury Board (CTB) Funding Strategy for Q3 and Q4 2025 introduces a segmented capital deployment framework designed to support Polygon’s next phase of growth: composable liquidity, strategic capital formation, and founder activation.

This Strategy consolidates and scopes three core Funds:

1. ***Liquidity & Incentives Fund*** to make Polygon chain the most liquid and composable DeFi environment on Ethereum.  
2. ***Capital Investments & Infrastructure Fund*** to back aligned teams and protocols with long-term upside, participation, and protocol \-level resilience.  
3. ***Founder Support Fund*** to accelerate the most promising founders through direct and programmatic capital, GTM, and community leverage.

The Strategy focuses on delivering impact through measurable outcomes, lean program overhead, and clear accountability. POL will be the default asset, with flexibility for USD when required. The collective budget for Season 3 is estimated at 80 million POL. 

Funds will operate under a defined reporting cadence and be reviewed for compliance against performance benchmarks.

This six-month Strategy aims to reduce friction, strengthen credibility, and scale the CTB’s ability to independently deploy capital with focus, speed, and strategic alignment.

## Specification

### Executive Strategy

The Community Treasury Board’s Q3/Q4 2025 Strategy introduces a *capital segmentation* model designed to professionalize treasury deployment, focus support on high impact actors, and drive measurable outcomes across the Polygon ecosystem.

This strategy was shaped by three core imperatives:

* ***Strategic Alignment*** – Ensure treasury capital reinforces Polygon’s flywheels: liquidity, infrastructure, founder ecosystems, and long-term aligned partners.  
* ***Operational Clarity*** – Replace diffuse, ad hoc grants with structured Funds and Programs that operate with clear mandates, funding rules, and accountability systems.  
* ***Credibility Through Results*** – Shift execution from allocators to operators: measuring outcomes, reporting consistently, and owning impact.

The three proposed Funds reflect this evolution:

* ***Liquidity & Incentives*** deploys POL to bootstrap deep, composable liquidity across Polygon’s DeFi ecosystem.  
* ***Capital Investments & Infrastructure*** backs aligned teams with skin in the game, and the structural rails that are mission-critical for their success.  
* ***Founder Support*** activates top talent through direct capital, programs, and community leverage.

As per PFP-3, each Fund will operate with a designated budget, structured sub-programs, and clear exclusions. Reporting will be quarterly at the Fund level and monthly for active Programs, with an emphasis on signal over surface metrics.

The CTB’s goal: deliver focused, transparent, founder-aligned capital deployment that compounds over time.

### Fund 1: Liquidity and Incentives

The Liquidity & Incentives Fund (L\&I) is a capital pool designed to make Polygon the most liquid and composable DeFi environment in the Ethereum ecosystem.  The Fund will achieve this by:

1. Deploying POL \-denominated capital to support TVL growth and protocol success across DeFi, payments, and emerging financial verticals such as RWAs  
2. Utilizing smart, retrievable deployments (e.g., seed liquidity, on-chain incentives) and yield strategies to sustain capital availability over time and minimize the direct sale of POL  
3. Ensuring accountability and impact through milestone \-based approvals and public governance and reporting

The goal of this Fund is to grow the network by increasing productive on-chain liquidity, transaction volumes and values, and DAU/MAU, while preserving and compounding Treasury resources, rather than depleting them.

#### Fund 1 Budget Allocation

25 million POL maximum

#### Fund Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/).

Quarterly fund reports will include:

* Summary of capital deployment (by program and total)  
* Yield earned from staking and lending  
* Liquidity or incentives outstanding  
* Capital efficiency highlights (e.g. POL working vs idle)  
* Snapshot of all Program statuses (active, completed, delayed)  
* Notes on upcoming proposals, renewals, or reallocations

#### Program 1.1 \- Incentives for Metrics

The Incentives for Metrics Program deploys POL to protocols on Polygon based on performance, measured through liquidity, usage, and retention milestones. This program does not fund deployments or marketing. It rewards sustained, verifiable impact, with capital released in stages tied to on-chain outcomes.

Protocols propose their own targets, which are approved by the Program Director. Incentives are streamed or vested upon meeting milestones, with no upfront payments unless secured by clawbacks. The goal is to build data on capital efficiency and drive durable growth across high-throughput DeFi protocols.

Example deals may include:

* A DEX receiving POL incentives for maintaining $5M+ TVL across stablecoin pairs and driving $10M+ monthly volume for three months  
* A perps platform earning incentives for achieving 3,000 DAU and sustaining above $25M monthly open interest  
* A stablecoin protocol rewarded for growing Polygon-native supply to $10M with \>50% liquidity retention over 60 days  
* A yield aggregator unlocking POL emissions only after passing specific usage and vault performance thresholds

Only a limited number of high-confidence protocols will be supported in this cycle.

##### Program 1.1 Budget

4 million POL per quarter

##### Program 1.1 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/).

Target outcomes include:

* Incentivize real on-chain behavior, measured via TVL, volume, retention, and network usage  
* Achieve sustained liquidity and engagement  
* Build comparative data on cost-per-outcome across verticals  
* Provide direct support to protocols scaling their presence on the Polygon chain

#### Program 1.2 \- Seed Liquidity Deployment Program

The Seed Liquidity Deployment Program allocates funding to early-stage or strategic protocols on Polygon chain to bootstrap liquidity, increase market confidence, and accelerate usage. POL (or USDC or other token) is deployed directly into on-chain pools or vaults and retrieved after a defined term.

This program does not provide grants or permanent liquidity. Deployments are milestone-informed and time-bound (60-180 days). The goal is to increase protocol surface area, deepen initial TVL, and catalyze usage without selling POL or issuing emissions.

Example deals may include:

* A new DEX seeded with 50K POL in a POL/USDC pool for 90 days, with minimum TVL and volume targets  
* A stablecoin issuer backstopped with POL in a lending protocol to support new mints  
* A structured yield vault launched with POL liquidity to build LP trust, withdrawn after protocol-specific KPIs

While targeting earlier-stage protocols, only a select number of deployments will be made each quarter.

##### Program 1.2 Budget

7 million POL per quarter

##### Program 1.2 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/) focusing on deployment outcomes and targets, capital efficiency metrics, and recommendations for retrieval, renewal, and/or program adjustments.

A monthly dashboard which includes a summary of active deployments, pool \-level metrics (etc.,TVL, volume, LPs), and unlock schedules.

### Fund 2: Capital Investments & Infrastructure 

#### Fund 2 Budget Allocation

40 million POL maximum

#### Fund Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/).

Quarterly fund reports will include:

* Summary of capital deployment (by program and total), including breakdown of investment vs strategic partnership deals  
* Deal-level status: active, completed, revoked, or pending  
* Exposure overview: equity, token, and non-dilutive structures, including any anticipated upside or rights secured  
* Capital efficiency insights: % deployed vs idle, average deal size, estimated return leverage (where applicable)  
* Snapshot of all active programs, plus pipeline notes for upcoming proposals or reallocations

There will also be additional qualitative information as needed to contextualize infrastructure investments that may not be well captured by more traditional quantitative metrics. 

#### Program 2.1 \- Aligned Capital Program

The Aligned Capital Program deploys POL-denominated capital into token or equity deals that offer long-term strategic upside and ecosystem alignment. This program does not offer grants or generalized ecosystem support. Capital is deployed only when there is clear alignment, measurable execution risk, and a defined path to token or equity participation. The goal is to fund builders directly and to build a portfolio of aligned teams whose success compounds network value and participation leverage over time.

Example deals may include:

* A real-world asset (RWA) protocol tokenizing short-term treasuries on Polygon, offering token exposure and revenue-sharing rights  
* A stablecoin protocol offering 3% token allocation to Polygon in exchange for launch support and liquidity partnership  
* An early-stage developer platform raising a SAFE \+ token side letter, with milestone-based capital release tied to usage growth  
* A payments project with strategic integrations across POS, receiving capital in return for ecosystem exclusivity and governance access

This program excludes:

* General-purpose grants with no upside or network participation  
* Sponsorships, marketing buys, or events without direct product or protocol involvement  
* Non-deploying teams without a clear path to Polygon chain integration or usage  
* Deals where Polygon is expected to lead the round, set terms, or act as sole anchor investor

Only a limited number of high-conviction investments will be made each quarter, with priority given to teams with proven execution, strong technical DNA, and clear ecosystem alignment.

##### Program 2.1 Budget

7 million POL per quarter

##### Program 2.1 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/) focusing on strategic alignment, participation exposure, and execution outcomes. Each report will include:

* Summary of new investments and total capital deployed  
* Status of active deals: milestone progress, unlocks, or delays  
* Participation exposure: token/equity rights secured, vesting status, governance roles  
* Recommendations for renewals, reallocations, or strategy adjustments

A monthly internal dashboard will be maintained for CTB and Treasury use, including:

* Deal-level tracking: active, completed, terminated  
* Capital deployed vs allocated  
* Vesting/unlock schedules for token and equity positions  
* Early risk flags, co-investor updates, and liquidity horizon notes

#### Program 2.2 \- Ecosystem Growth Program 

The Ecosystem Growth Program allocates POL-denominated capital to support execution-focused teams that are strategically valuable to Polygon, but do not offer token or equity participation. These deals are designed to increase traction, distribution, and surface area on Polygon.

This program does not provide open-ended grants or passive sponsorships. Capital is deployed under milestone-informed, time-bound terms, with clear usage goals and predefined exit conditions. Deployments are reversible and must demonstrate measurable impact.

The goal is to create lightweight, high-leverage capital flows that unlock execution, especially where speed, timing, or strategic opportunity outweigh the need for financial return.

Example deals may include:

* A payments SDK team integrating with major POS systems, with POL-based funding tied to live deployments  
* A wallet provider supporting Polygon-native onboarding, with usage milestones tied to funded marketing and referral mechanics  
* A stablecoin issuer expanding into new regions, with milestone-based funding to drive TVL, mints, and user retention  
* A developer tools platform funded to expand Polygon compatibility, with capital released upon delivery of live integrations and usage benchmarks

This program excludes:

* Unstructured grants with no clear ROI or strategic tie-in  
* Events, marketing spends, or ecosystem awareness campaigns without measurable usage outcomes  
* Teams without a deployed product or confirmed Polygon roadmap  
* Any deals where capital would be used primarily for fundraising, rather than product or traction outcomes  
* Requests for retroactive support or reimbursement

Only a limited number of high-impact, non-dilutive deals will be approved each quarter. Priority is given to teams with strong execution history, live deployments, and a proven ability to move the ecosystem forward.

##### Program 2.2 Budget

7 million POL per quarter

##### Program 2.2 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/) focusing on milestone delivery, on-chain outcomes, and capital efficiency. Each report will include:

* Summary of new deployments and total capital allocated  
* Status of active deals: milestone progress, unlocks, or delays  
* Execution outcomes: usage growth, integrations, TVL, or retention against targets  
* Recommendations for renewals, retrievals, reallocations, or program scope adjustments

A monthly internal dashboard will be maintained for CTB and Treasury use, including:

* Deal-level tracking: active, completed, or withdrawn  
* Capital deployed vs allocated  
* Unlock schedules and retrieval deadlines  
* Early risk flags, underperformance alerts, and protocol-level summaries

#### Program 2.3 \- Infrastructure

The Infrastructure Program funds ongoing infrastructure commitments that support and grow Polygon, such as RPC endpoints, indexers, block explorers, testnets, monitoring, and security audits. Infrastructure Providers may be  sourced from the Polygon Solution Providers Network; however this is not an explicit requirement. Alternative examples below: 

\- Company that is selling a popular product that Polygon Labs wants to offer to developers or users without upfront or recurring costs on Polygon protocols  
\- RaaS providers who want to offer competitive pricing to incentivize a developer to build on Polygon’s CDK stack;   
\- End to end devshops who will be developing new use cases on Polygon protocols

Eligible commitments include:

* RPC Consortium: Funding to maintain a multi-provider RPC network  
* Indexing & Archive Nodes: Support for decentralized indexer deployments and archive-node backups  
* Security Audits: Engagements with top-tier firms for protocol/client audits, tied to published remediation plans  
* Explorer Features: Enhancements to block explorers (deep linking, analytics pages, API endpoints)

The majority of this Program’s budget will go towards the ongoing payment of existing infrastructure contracts.  A small reserve (\~10–15% of this program’s allocation) remains available for emergent, high-priority infrastructure needs not yet under contract.

##### Program 2.3 Budget

5 million POL per quarter

##### Program 2.3 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/) focusing on milestone delivery, on-chain outcomes, and capital efficiency. Each report will include:

* Net New Deals: count, terms, and POL committed  
* Adoption Signals: SDK/API call volume, explorer session counts, testnet participation

A monthly internal dashboard will be maintained for CTB and Treasury use, including:

* Contract Pipeline: active, pending, and buffer-funded requests  
* Payment Status: invoices processed vs. outstanding  
* Budget Burn vs. Deliverables: POL drawdown against milestone completion

### Fund 3: Founder Support 

The Founder Support Fund (FSF) is a flexible, execution-focused capital pool designed to reduce founder friction and scale activity across the Polygon ecosystem. Rather than funding ideas or proposal decks, FSF deploys capital, services, and support infrastructure to unlock real world momentum for builders.

The Fund will achieve this by:

* Deploying POL-denominated capital across subsidized services, milestone-based funding, tactical contributor payments, and targeted contributor support  
* Activating early execution through fast, reversible funding loops that reward traction  
* Scaling thematic and vertical ecosystems (e.g. Payments, RWAs) by funding community evangelists and domain-specific contributors  
* Empowering the Treasury to identify and resource top builders and contributors without requiring equity, tokens, or formal affiliations (though without precluding these tools)

The goal of this Fund is to create a durable founder ecosystem through lean, modular programs that support building, launching, scaling, and connecting.

#### Fund 3 Budget Allocation

15 million POL 

#### Fund Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the [Polygon Community Forum](https://forum.polygon.technology/).

Quarterly fund reports will include:

* Summary of capital deployment (by use case)  
* Founder and contributor highlights (quantitative \+ narrative)  
* Execution outcomes (launches, usage, traction, GTM)  
* Layer-by-layer capital efficiency and retention metrics  
* Recommendations for renewal or scale

#### Program 3.1 \- Support Layer Program

The Support Layer Program deploys POL-denominated capital, services, and contributor infrastructure to accelerate execution across high-leverage founders building on Polygon. The program supports tactical needs across all stages  from audits to GTM to community signal, with an emphasis on speed, modularity, and measurable outcomes.

This program does not offer open-ended grants or passive sponsorships. Instead, it funds tightly scoped support across two structured layers:

* The **Foundational Layer** unblocks baseline execution for founders, such as through audit subsidies, travel stipends for event access, and user and founder research funding.  
* The **Execution Layer** accelerates founders to tangible milestones, such as using ‘fast grants’ for MVPs, embedded contributor programs, and tactical cofunding (e.g. accelerators, hackathons) where founder impact is direct and measurable.

Example support may include:

* A stablecoin team receives a 5K POL fast grant to launch their MVP and onboard first users  
* A Payments evangelist is contracted to support founder onboarding, integrations, and showcase content for 90 days  
* A physical hub receives 100K POL to fund 6 months of programming for Polygon-native founders, with KPIs tied to number of PoS deployments, traction milestones, and downstream capital raised  
* A founder receives a subsidized audit \+ travel support to attend and speak at ETHCC  
* A scout in LatAm runs a program to identify RWA founders, earning POL for signal surfaced  
* A Network School candidate receives POL to cover tuition and serves as a regional point-of-contact post-graduation

This program excludes:

* Unstructured or retroactive grants with no traction or PoS alignment  
* Awareness-only efforts through sponsorships  
* Founders with no working product, team, or public roadmap  
* Full-time contractors or team budgets not tied to specific outputs

##### Program 3.1 Budget

6 million POL per quarter

##### Program 3.1 Impact Measures & Reporting Cadence

A quarterly report published in the Polygon Community Forum, focused on founder outcomes, contributor performance, and capital efficiency. Each report will include:

* Summary of deployments and total capital allocated  
* Outcomes by support layer (Foundational vs. Execution)  
* Execution metrics: product launches, onchain usage, integrations, traction milestones  
* Contributor outcomes: scout activity, evangelist output, community activation  
* Capital breakdown by support type (e.g. audits, travel, fast grants, contractors)  
* Recommendations for renewal, termination, or scale-up

A monthly internal dashboard will be maintained for CTB and Treasury use, including:

* Deployment and contributor tracker  
* Capital deployed vs. allocated  
* Retrieval timelines, extension requests, or rollover recommendations  
* Underperformance alerts, deviation flags, or contributor gaps
